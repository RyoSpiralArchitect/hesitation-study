# Research notes

For new research waves, run both English and Japanese on matched items. Freeze the study instruments and describe any departures in timing, translation, judging or sample alignment. Keep English as the primary documentation language and include a Japanese summary. Accumulate brief dated Research Notes, with the hypothesis, contrasts, observed utterances, denominators, uncertainty and links to exact artifacts. Focus on observations across small experiments. Keep an explicit distinction between answer behavior and inferences about internal states.
